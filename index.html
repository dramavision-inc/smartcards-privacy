Smart Cards Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Cards and study activity

Smart Cards does not require an account. Your saved decks, cards, study progress, and app settings are stored on your device. Smart Cards does not sync this library to our servers. If you export or share a deck yourself, the service or person you choose may receive a copy.

Cloud AI card generation

When you use cloud AI generation in Smart Cards Pro, the text you submit is sent to our backend hosted by Supabase. The backend passes that text to the Gemini model through Google Cloud Vertex AI to generate flashcards. The request does not upload your saved card library or study history. If you do not want to send text, avoid cloud AI generation. You can still create and study cards manually. Please do not submit confidential information or personal information you do not want shared.

The generation service receives the request's IP address for rate limiting and may receive the app version and other standard request information. The service and its providers may process or retain limited request information for operations, security, and abuse prevention. Google Cloud also describes how its caching and retention controls work. We do not promise that using cloud AI generation leaves no data off your device.

Purchases

Apple processes the one-time Smart Cards Pro purchase under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app uses RevenueCat to check Pro access when it starts, show purchase options, and verify or restore purchases. RevenueCat uses an identifier generated by RevenueCat for this app and processes purchase and entitlement information, which can include a store receipt. Smart Cards does not receive your payment card details.

Managing your information

You can delete saved cards and decks from the app. Uninstalling the app removes its on-device storage, but does not delete exports, device backups, or data held by service providers. Contact us to ask about access to or deletion of information handled by Smart Cards, or if you believe a child submitted personal information.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@dramavision.top.

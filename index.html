Smart Cards - Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

Overview

Smart Cards is designed with privacy first. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

Smart Cards does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your flashcards, decks, and study progress are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

No Analytics

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks.

No Account Required

You do not need to create an account to use Smart Cards. There is no login, no cloud sync, and no server communication.

Data Storage

All data — including your flashcard decks, study history, and settings — is stored on your device using local storage (SQLite). If you delete the app, all data is permanently removed from your device.

In-App Purchases

Smart Cards offers a one-time in-app purchase (SmartCards Pro) processed through Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store any payment information. Apple handles all purchase transactions according to their privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

Smart Cards does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. The AI card generation feature processes text locally and does not send your notes to any external server.

Children's Privacy

Smart Cards does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: privacy@dramavision.top
